# tessera-v2-scales

## Source

TESSERA v2 embeddings (`v2-2B-L~beta1`, 2024) on Source Cooperative, with the CRS and transform of each tile from its landmask GeoTIFF.

## Sample

The per-pixel scale of the codes, 512×512 pixels at 10 m, cropped as in `tessera-v2-codes`.

## Representation

Scales as published, float32.

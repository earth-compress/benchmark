# emit-l2a-285

## Source

Earth Engine `NASA/EMIT/L2A/RFL`, EMIT L2A surface reflectance, orthorectified on a 0.000542 degree grid.

## Sample

285 bands of 128×128 pixels on the native grid of the scene.

## Representation

Reflectance as published, float32. Windows hold no fill value.

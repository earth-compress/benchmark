# cop-dem-glo30

## Source

Copernicus DEM GLO-30 COGs in the AWS open data bucket `copernicus-dem-30m`.

## Sample

512×512 posts at 1 arc second, inside one tile.

## Representation

Elevation in m as published, float32.

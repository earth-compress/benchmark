# cop-dem-glo30

## Source

Copernicus DEM GLO-30 COGs in the AWS open data bucket `copernicus-dem-30m`.

## Sample

512×512 posts at 1 arc second, inside one tile. The manifest gives the CRS, the geotransform, and the time of every sample.

## Representation

Elevation in m as published, float32. The values are not whole metres.

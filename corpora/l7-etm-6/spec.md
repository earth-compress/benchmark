# l7-etm-6

## Source

Earth Engine Landsat Collection 2 Level-1 assets, Tier 1 or Tier 2 as listed in the manifest.

## Sample

6 reflective ETM+ bands (B1 to B5, B7) of 512×512 pixels at 30 m. The manifest gives the CRS, the geotransform, and the time of every sample.

## Representation

Level-1 digital numbers as published. NoData is 0. uint8.

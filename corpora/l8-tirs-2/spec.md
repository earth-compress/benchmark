# l8-tirs-2

## Source

Earth Engine Landsat Collection 2 Level-1 assets, Tier 1 or Tier 2 as listed in the manifest.

## Sample

The TIRS bands B10 and B11, 512×512 pixels on the 30 m delivery grid. The manifest gives the CRS, the geotransform, and the time of every sample.

## Representation

Level-1 digital numbers as published. NoData is 0. uint16.

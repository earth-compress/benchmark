# l7-etm-tir-2

## Source

Earth Engine Landsat Collection 2 Level-1 assets, Tier 1 or Tier 2 as listed in the manifest.

## Sample

The ETM+ thermal band at low and high gain (B6_VCID_1, B6_VCID_2), 512×512 pixels on the 30 m delivery grid. The manifest gives the CRS, the geotransform, and the time of every sample.

## Representation

Level-1 digital numbers as published. NoData is 0. uint8.

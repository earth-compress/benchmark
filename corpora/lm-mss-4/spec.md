# lm-mss-4

## Source

Earth Engine Landsat Collection 2 Level-1 assets, Tier 1 or Tier 2 as listed in the manifest.

## Sample

4 MSS bands (green, red, two near infrared) of 512×512 pixels at 60 m, from Landsat 1 to 5. The manifest gives the CRS, the geotransform, and the time of every sample.

## Representation

Level-1 digital numbers as published. NoData is 0. uint8.

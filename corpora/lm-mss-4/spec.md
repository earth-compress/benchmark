# lm-mss-4

## Source

Earth Engine Landsat Collection 2 Level-1, Tier 2.

## Sample

4 MSS bands (green, red, two near infrared) of 512×512 pixels at 60 m, from Landsat 1 to 5.

## Representation

uint8 digital numbers as published. NoData is 0.

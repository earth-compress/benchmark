# esa-wc-v200

## Source

ESA WorldCover 10 m 2021 v200 COGs in the AWS open data bucket `esa-worldcover`.

## Sample

512×512 pixels at 1/12000 degree, inside one tile.

## Representation

Land-cover class codes as published, uint8. NoData is 0.

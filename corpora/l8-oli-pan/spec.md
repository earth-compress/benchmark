# l8-oli-pan

## Source

Earth Engine Landsat Collection 2 Level-1 assets, Tier 1 or Tier 2 as listed in the manifest.

## Sample

The OLI panchromatic band B8, 1024×1024 pixels at 15 m. The manifest gives the CRS, the geotransform, and the time of every sample.

## Representation

Level-1 digital numbers as published. NoData is 0. uint16.

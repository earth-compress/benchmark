s1-grd-iw-2

Sentinel-1 GRD IW, VV and VH backscatter, linear.

Source

Earth Engine COPERNICUS/S1_GRD_FLOAT, Sentinel-1 GRD IW after the Earth Engine preprocessing (thermal noise removal, radiometric calibration, terrain correction).

Sample

VV and VH, 512×512 pixels at 10 m on the native UTM grid of the scene.

Representation

Linear backscatter coefficient sigma0 as stored, float32. Windows hold no masked pixel.

Licence

Copernicus Sentinel data 2023, under the Copernicus data policy. Required attribution: "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2023."
